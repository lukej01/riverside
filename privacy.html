Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Riverside Pizza ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at riversidepizza.net. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly through this website. When you place an order, you are directed to third-party platforms — Toast Tab for carryout orders and DoorDash for delivery — each of which has its own privacy policy and data practices.

We use Google Fonts to serve typography on this site. Google Fonts may log your IP address and browser information when fonts are loaded from Google's servers. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Third-Party Ordering Platforms

Online carryout orders are processed through Toast Tab. Online delivery orders are processed through DoorDash. When you use these services, the respective third party collects and processes your order details, payment information, and personal data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Toast Privacy Policy
  • DoorDash Privacy Policy

Riverside Pizza does not receive or store your payment card information.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own. Third-party services such as Google Fonts and embedded maps (OpenStreetMap) may set cookies or use browser storage as described in their respective privacy policies.

Contact Forms

If you submit our contact or job application form, the information you provide (name, email, message) is sent to the Riverside Pizza management team for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page or by calling any of our 8 Metro Atlanta locations.
