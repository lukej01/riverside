# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Families and individuals across Metro Atlanta (Lawrenceville, Suwanee, Grayson, Johns Creek, Cumming, Dacula, Hamilton Mill) deciding where to order pizza. Most arrive on mobile at lunch or in the evening, often comparing against chain delivery apps. Secondary: office managers planning group catering for 20-300+ people.

## Product Purpose
Convert skeptical visitors into first-time carryout orders. Build enough trust in the family-owned brand that someone who found Riverside on Google chooses it over Dominos. Secondary: drive catering inquiries and reinforce loyalty with regulars. Success is an order placed or a location phone call made.

## Brand Personality
Honest, local, craft. Warm but not folksy. Confident without being corporate. The brand earns trust through specifics — named ingredients, real years, real founders — not adjectives.

## Anti-references
- Dominos / Papa John's / Pizza Hut: chain-corporate, discount-first, aggressive promotions
- Generic Yelp-era restaurant sites: stock food photos, template grid layouts, no personality
- Overly-rustic "artisan pizza" branding: distressed textures, mason jars, chalkboard fonts, forced nostalgia
- Sterile white SaaS-clean sites: too cold and polished for a neighborhood family restaurant

## Design Principles
1. Show, don't tell: use photography and specifics (Stanislaus by name, Hormel by name, All Trumps flour) instead of adjectives like "fresh" and "quality"
2. Earn trust through precision: real numbers, real years, real founders — every claim must be provable
3. Local pride, not local kitsch: family-owned without being folksy; 27 years without drowning in nostalgia
4. Friction-free path to order: every section has a visible next step toward ordering or finding a location
5. Consistent across 8: the site should feel as consistent as the pizza — same standard at every touchpoint

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Focus traps on mobile nav overlay. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected on all pages. Minimum 44px touch targets on all interactive elements.
