For Everyone at the Table

Gluten Free Options.

We offer a gluten-free crust so everyone at the table can enjoy Riverside Pizza together.

Real crust. Real pizza.

Our gluten-free crust is made from a certified rice flour blend — carefully sourced and tested before it ever enters our kitchen. It's the same 10-inch personal size at every one of our 8 locations.

Load it with any topping from our menu: the same Stanislaus sauce, the same whole-milk mozzarella, the same fresh ingredients. Gluten-free doesn't mean a diminished experience.

Available every day, at every Riverside location, for dine-in or carryout. Just ask your server or note it in your online order.

Allergen Notice

While we are proud to offer a certified gluten-free crust, our kitchens are not dedicated gluten-free environments. Pizzas are prepared in the same area as our traditional dough, and shared equipment is used throughout the kitchen. We cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination. If you have celiac disease or a severe gluten allergy, please consult with your server and use your own judgment before ordering.
